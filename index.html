Dune Drifter Privacy Policy

Developer: Thin Air Capital
App: Dune Drifter (com.thinaircapital.dunedrifter)
Effective date: October 1, 2026

Dune Drifter does not collect, sell, or share any personal information with us or with advertisers. It has no ads, no accounts of its own, and no analytics or tracking.

What the app stores

Your game progress (scores, best distances, badges, missions, and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or using Reset Progress in the game, deletes it.

What the app does not do

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game sends your unlocked achievements and your best distances (for the leaderboards) to Google Play Games so they appear in your Play Games profile. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Thin Air Capital does not receive or store this information. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings.

Device features

The app uses your phone's vibration for game effects. This works entirely on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
